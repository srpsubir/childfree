🧠 Project Memory: Kindred (V2)
🎯 Product Vision
Kindred is a high-integrity, child-free community platform. It is designed as a "Certainty Archive"—a tool for women to find partners and community without the risk of "the bait-and-switch" or ambivalent mind-changing.

Core Strategy: Women-first marketplace. Build the "Sanctuary of Certainty" for women; men will follow for the high-value community.

Aesthetic: "The Gallery." Minimalist, high-end editorial. Sharp 0px corners, Charcoal (#121212) and Champagne (#F7E7CE) palette.

🏗️ System Architecture: The "Certainty" Loop
The Audit: Users enter an @ig_handle. This serves as a "Lifestyle Resume" (Proof of lifestyle).

The Pillar: Selection of Truth, Autonomy, or Legacy as the motive for child-free life.

The Pledge: A mandatory 3-second long-press "contract" confirming 100% certainty in the child-free decision.

Strategic Friction: A 7.5s "Integrity Pulse" that filters for ambiguity and assigns a table.

The Table: Assigned community match (e.g., The Mitte Table) with a specific track manifesto.

🛠️ Design System (The Gallery)
Typography: Playfair Display (Headers), Inter (Technical/Body).

Corners: rounded-none (0px). Dead sharp.

Colors: * Background: #121212 (Deep Charcoal)

Text: #F5F5F7 (Off-White)

Accents: #F7E7CE (Champagne Gold)
